## Overview

**The problem.** Bots (testclients) normally idle or run their own simple AI. For debugging hitboxes, timing a peek, or walking through a strat, you often want them to do exactly what you do - same move, look, and fire - without scripting every step.

**What we improved.** With `bot_mimic 1`, connected bots replay a human player's usercmds. They move and shoot like you for debug and strat work. You can pick which player to copy, fan bots with a yaw offset, or freeze them on your current position and stance.

| Cvar | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bot_mimic` | `0` | `1` = bots copy a human's usercmds |
| `bot_mimic_target` | `-1` | Client slot to copy. `-1` = first active human |
| `bot_mimic_yaw_offset` | `0` | Extra yaw degrees per bot index. `0` = every bot stacks on the same inputs |
| `bot_mimic_freeze` | `0` | `1` = pin bots to the source player's origin and stance when freeze turns on |

## Stock

Official CoD4x testclients get empty or stub usercmds from the bot path. They do not record a human player's commands or feed those commands back into `SV_ClientThink`. There is no mimic / freeze / yaw-fan control.

## What changed

While `bot_mimic` is on, each human usercmd is stored in a short ring history (64 commands per slot) via `SV_BotMimic_Record`. On the bot update path, each bot finds a source player (`bot_mimic_target`, or the first non-bot `CS_ACTIVE` client), then replays the unread history into `SV_ClientThink` with remapped `serverTime` so the bot stays in sync with server time.

`bot_mimic_yaw_offset` adds `ordinal * offset` to yaw (bot ordinal among connected bots). Use it to spread clones around a shared aim. Weapon / offhand indices that the bot does not own fall back to the bot's current weapon.

`bot_mimic_freeze 1` captures the source player's origin, viewangles, stance flags, lean, and view height once when freeze turns on, then pins every mimicking bot there each frame (velocity cleared). Turning freeze off clears the pin and resets mimic cursors.
